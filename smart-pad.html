More than a landing surface.

Communications & local coordination

Discover nearby aircraft, exchange capabilities, sequence requests and publish pad status. A local coordinator would keep an auditable record of the exchange.

Research: radio transport, identity, cybersecurity, lost-link behavior and aircraft adapters.

ARRIVE

Ask before approaching

The aircraft announces its intent. The node checks the requested pad class and availability, then responds with a hold or reservation.

LAND

Coordinate the final metres

Guidance references and landing-area sensing could support alignment and pad-status updates. The aircraft’s controller performs the landing.

TURN AROUND

Hand over to ground services

After touchdown and propulsion-safe confirmation, the node could coordinate compatible charging, inspection or payload handling.

What we can show today
