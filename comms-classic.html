What this demonstration represents

GroundNode coordination is automatic within this synthetic scenario. The pilot accepts or declines reservations and separately engages illustrative flight guidance. Obstruction is a scenario input, not a real camera detection. The cockpit display is a Vahnim interface concept, not an FMS or certified flight instrument. Preset reviews do not establish operating permission. Pad authorization does not replace airspace or ATC authorization. Open the integration lab for detailed review and failure exercises.
