U.S. requirements reference & research boundaries

FAA-informed design, with applicability still to establish

The small-UAS and powered-lift paths require different operating frameworks. The operator review records an attestation; it does not validate certificates, issue airspace permission or establish compliance.

FAA small-UAS guidance · Powered-lift framework · Vertiport design guidance

An original Vahnim concept workflow. It is not CPDLC, does not connect to FAA systems, and has no FAA approval. Design dimensions and timings remain illustrative.

Concept protocol · equipped aircraft use a proposed adapter; visitors require a supported operator channel and review. Detected aircraft without a channel are tracked only. All identities, inspection results and deliveries in this exercise are simulated. No production SDK, AI vision or universal aircraft compatibility is claimed.
