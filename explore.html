Cockpit encounter

A guided practice landing. You sit in the air taxi's cockpit seat while a coach tells you, in plain words, what is happening and what to press next.

Try
Follow the coach through a full landing, then press "Block the pad" and watch the plane go around and pick another pad.
Good for
First-time viewers, demos to non-technical people.

Smart pad

A landing pad that thinks: lights that guide the aircraft, sensors that spot trouble, power for charging, and a radio link to talk to the aircraft.

Try
Click any part of the pad or the list on the right, then press the exploded view.
Good for
Explaining why the ground needs to be part of the system.
Open the smart pad → Advanced

Integration lab

A workshop for harder situations between aircraft and ground: a visitor aircraft from another operator, an unknown aircraft, offers that run out of time, and lost links.

Try
Connect the visitor aircraft, then break the link halfway through a landing.
Good for
Engineers checking that the rules hold up when things go wrong.

Words you will see

UAM
Urban air mobility aircraft: an electric air taxi that carries passengers.
UAV
A small drone with no one on board, used for deliveries and inspections.
GroundNode
The landing pad and the computer that talks to aircraft and looks after them.
Clearance
Permission to land or take off on one named pad, valid for a short time.
Read-back
The pilot repeats a short code from the clearance so both sides know they agree.
Verified message
A message with a check that proves who sent it and that nobody changed it.
Go-around
Cancelling a landing at the last moment: climb, hold and try again.
Hover
Staying still in the air, like a helicopter.
